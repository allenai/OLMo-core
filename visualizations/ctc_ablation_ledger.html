Chunked Attention Ledger

What the document-chunked attention ablations currently measure, which cells are still empty, and which published-looking numbers are artifacts rather than results. Every figure here is read from a grade JSON, not carried forward in prose.

Two results firmed up overnight: the chunked-vs-dense gap now narrows monotonically across three model sizes, and mask-mixing turns out to be what makes chunked attention learnable on every O(N²) task measured — while doing essentially nothing on retrieval.

03  ablation

How much does mask-mixing actually buy?

Pure-chunked runs — no p_standard curriculum at all — against the mask-mixed and dense arms at 4B. There is no single answer. Mask-mixing is the difference between working and not working on the O(N²) task, worth roughly nothing on retrieval, and at short context it is actively negative on the O(N·M) task.

The zeros are real, not broken evals: every one has parse_rate 1.0, a verified non-zero chunk-mask application count, and a training CE that descended normally. Without the curriculum the model simply never learns these tasks under the chunk mask.

All five tasks now have full ladders, and the pattern is unambiguous. What mask-mixing is worth tracks the task's complexity class and nothing else: +0.79 and +0.47 on the two O(N²) tasks at 2k, −0.07 on the O(N·M) task at 2k, and +0.02 on the O(N) task. Re-order without it sits at exactly chance (−0.002); contradiction at 0.0007; outlier survives at 0.169, badly wounded but not dead. So mask-mixing is not a general improvement to chunked attention — it is close to the entire difference between learning and not learning pair-finding, and close to a no-op on retrieval.

One caveat on the O(N²) rows: the advantage shrinks at long context, from +0.79 to +0.11 on outlier between 2k and 32k. That is not mask-mixing failing — it is the mask-mixed arm itself falling toward the floor, so there is less left to rescue. Read the middle rungs, not the ends.

05  architecture

Does the backbone matter, or only the task?

Every headline number in the suite is measured on Qwen3.5, a 3:1 Gated-DeltaNet:full hybrid where the chunk mask only ever touches one layer in four. The cross-family question is whether the dense-vs-chunked gap is a property of the task or of that backbone. Two families are now measured on the same ladder, and the answer is not the simple one.

The two chunked curves cross. OLMo-3-7B's chunked arm is far stronger at short context — 0.972 against Qwen's 0.861 at 2.5k, a 0.11 lead — and then falls faster, ending below it at 16k (0.661 vs 0.759). The dense arms, by contrast, sit within 0.03 of each other at every rung. So the backbone barely matters for dense attention and matters a great deal for how chunked attention degrades with length: Qwen's Gated-DeltaNet hybrid starts worse and decays more gracefully; OLMo-3's sliding-window hybrid starts near-dense and falls off a cliff. Reporting a single "chunked gap" per family would hide this entirely.

The Olmo-Hybrid chunked row is NOT a clean backbone result — it is an underfitting result. Its chunked arm reached a final training CE of 0.958 where OLMo-3's chunked arm on the identical task, data and step count reached 0.171 (dense arms: 0.150 and 0.042). It never fit the training data, so 0.113 at 2.5k measures that, not what a linear-attention backbone can represent under a chunk mask. It is also NOT the Llama-8B failure pattern — there CE stalled at 1.807 having barely moved, here it descended 3.04 → 0.958. The honest reading is "the GDN backbone made the chunked contradiction objective much harder to optimize at this recipe", which is a training-dynamics claim needing a longer or re-tuned run to separate from a capacity claim. The hybrid's qdmatch chunked arm trained to CE 0.156 and is the control: if it evaluates normally, the backbone is fine in general and contradiction-chunked is specifically hard.

These are not the numbers in olmo3_dense_vs_chunked.md. That page reports OLMo-3 at 0.829/0.767 at 2k, measured on the contradiction ladder; this table is contradiction_iid, the ladder matching the training shard. Same checkpoints, different corpus, ~0.14 apart at 2k. The old page is not wrong, it answers a different question — but the two must never be mixed in one figure.

07  harness

Bugs found while collecting this

Each of these produced, or would have produced, a well-formed number that was wrong. They are listed because the fix matters less than knowing which past results were exposed.

Chunked evaluation silently unmasked under tensor parallelism

The chunk mask is an in-process monkey-patch relying on VLLM_ENABLE_V1_MULTIPROCESSING=0. With TP>1 vLLM spawns worker subprocesses the patch never reaches, so the job completes, reports mode=chunked, and publishes the dense number. Caught because a control rung returned 0.9165619047619048 — bit-identical to the same checkpoint scored dense, with patch_debug.calls=0. 21 jobs cancelled, one published result deleted. Chunked now forces TP=1 and the pipeline refuses to publish when the mask applied zero times.

Every non-4B eval rebuilt the model at 4B

BASE_MODEL_ID was hardcoded to Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B-Base, and the export loads the checkpoint into a model built from it. The 0.8B contradiction eval died on a [248320, 1024] vs [248320, 2560] mismatch after 28 minutes of environment build. It failed loudly, which is the good case — but it is why that ladder is still empty.

Olmo-Hybrid markers arrived at one-sixth strength

The converted base passed the marker cosine gate (−0.0230, fully distinguishable) and failed the norm gate: doc_start and doc_end at 0.15× and 0.16× the median trained-row norm. RMSNorm rescales every position to unit norm, so those rows arrive as full-strength noise at every marker. Training from that base would have produced a clean-looking null. Repaired from the trained «/» delimiters to 0.78× and 0.60×; re-audit passes both gates.

has_fla() is not a usable gate

It is fla is not None behind a bare try: import fla, so it passes on a partial install. It returned True on a job whose next line died with No module named 'fla.modules'. Gates now import the exact symbol the model needs.
