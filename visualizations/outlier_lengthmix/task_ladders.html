Task Scaling Ladders

Reading a panel. Colour is the eval rung, so the length trend reads down the colours. Solid line + filled dot = dense; dashed line + hollow dot = sparse-landmark, so the architecture gap is the distance between the two lines of the same colour. Vertical bars are ±1 binomial SE at eval_size 500 (±.022 near .5) — a gap smaller than two of them is not a result. Curves span only the budgets actually trained; nothing here is extrapolated.

Length scaling: FLOPs to reach 0.5 vs context length

Each point is a measured rung converted twice: the fitted data law gives the tokens needed for 0.5 at that length, and the FLOP model converts tokens to compute (6N for parameters plus attention over L, with sparse paying 1/64 of the attention term; only 8 of 32 layers are full attention in this GDN hybrid). Filled circles are those measured-rung points, hollow squares are the extrapolation to 64k/128k/256k/1M, and β is the fitted slope of log-FLOPs on log-length. Where sparse's β is smaller, the two lines must cross — the green rule marks it.

Only tasks whose rungs actually bracket 0.5 appear here: a rung already above the target at its smallest budget, or one needing more than 20× the largest budget we ran, is dropped rather than fitted, because in both cases the "budget" would be the curve running away rather than a measurement.
