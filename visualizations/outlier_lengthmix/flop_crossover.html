Dense vs Sparse per FLOP

dense   sparse-landmark   crossover. Filled circles and solid curves are measured; dashed is extrapolation, run deliberately far (to 1e26 FLOPs) so a crossover shows up even when it is nowhere near anything we could run.

Why the FLOP axis barely moves the answer. Qwen3.5 interleaves Gated DeltaNet and full attention 3:1, so only 8 of 32 layers pay a quadratic cost. At our training mixes attention is roughly a tenth of the compute, and sparse-landmark's 1/64 attention discount buys only: absence 1.15×, contradiction 1.11×, grouping 1.11×, nq 1.11×, oolong 1.10×, outlier 1.11×, qdmatch_nq 1.11×, reorder 1.06×, textgroups 1.06×, xabsence 1.13× fewer FLOPs per token. Sparsity is a large lever only when attention dominates the bill, which needs a much longer training mix than any arm here used. So a curve that loses on tokens loses on FLOPs too, by almost the same margin.
