Setup

Model. Qwen3.5-4B (32 blocks: Gated DeltaNet linear attention, full attention at blocks 3, 7, …, 31), olmo-core, bf16, parameters frozen; one teacher-forced forward + backward of the answer cross-entropy (no-cot) per row on one A100 (Lambda node02).

Rows. mt4 rows: mt4s1952 rows 1464–1527 of contradiction, nq, outlier, oolong (held out from every mt4 run up to s1464; eval_size = 64 rows per task). mt11 rows: mt11s720 rows 480–543 of all 11 tasks (held out from the base, mt11 runs up to s480 and every mt4 run; mt11 rows use the setA prompt format, so they are out of format for mt4-trained checkpoints, drawn dashed).

Checkpoints. Repaired base q35-4b-base-markerfix; mt4 dense LR 5e-6 after 31 / 61 / ~122 / 366 steps and LR 2e-6 after 366; mt11 dense LR 5e-6 after 165 / 330; online routers otr10M8 (mt4, 366 steps, keep 10%, drop before block 8) and otr20M8 (mt11, 330 steps).

Per token t, block b. G = ‖dL/dhb,t‖ at each block input; residual updates of the mixer and the FFN; first-order skip attribution ⟨dL/dh, Δh⟩; the token's share of the parameter gradient at each output projection (‖δt‖·‖xt‖, the exact norm of its rank-1 term); attention mass from the answer-predicting positions at the 8 full-attention blocks. Plus exact per-block parameter-gradient norms.

Limits

All rankings are full-context oracles: a router scores tokens from early hidden states and, in a pyramid, on an already-dropped context. The split-fidelity curve counts gradient energy, not direction; dropping also changes the kept tokens' gradients (the grad-sim work found that is the larger effect). The ablation is forward-only (ΔCE at a fixed checkpoint), not a training run. 64 rows per task is well below the 500-example default; differences under about ±0.03 in shares are noise. Gold flags come from gold_fingerprints.json; oolong and reorder have none.
