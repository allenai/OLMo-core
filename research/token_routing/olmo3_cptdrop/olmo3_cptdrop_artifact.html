Token-dropping continued pretraining on OLMo 3 7B: an empirical screen

Starting from a 7B base that has only ever seen 8k contexts, does dropping tokens during long-context continued pretraining reach dense's dev loss for less compute, at every budget, not only the smallest?

screen answered  No token-dropping arm beats dense at any budget. The best multiplier is 0.6× (random-block drop at the 8-step budget), against a 10–20× target and a ~4× bar from softdetach.

Dense CPT from OLMo 3 stage 2 at LR 2e-5 saturates within about 12 steps (6M tokens): 4 → 12 steps moves dev CE 0.012 nats, 12 → 240 steps only 0.015. Every drop arm lands above the dense curve at matched FLOPs, from 4-step budgets up to 48: random 20% of blocks (m 0.2–0.6), sd20-style slots instead of removal (identical to removal within 0.0005), first+last 25% (≤0.3), first+last 10% (worse with more training). Context gain, which isolates long-context learning, ranks the arms the same way. The curriculum arm ties dense or slightly trails it (~1.05× at 120, ~0.8× at 480). At 64k the picture is the same: first+last 25% scores 1.751 against dense 1.721 at 120 steps. The levers that did move the dense curve were the learning rate (1e-5 ≈ 2× the compute efficiency of 2e-5 at 120 steps) and, at the lowest budget, a smaller batch.

Why this differs from softdetach's ~4×: that dense curve kept falling 0.046 nats per decade of compute (Qwen3.5-4B, already long-trained); here it falls about 0.011, so there is no room below dense for a cheaper arm to sit in. A stronger test needs a target where dense keeps improving for longer: longer context (64k / 128k eval) or a base further from the data.

Why this experiment

Earlier token-drop CPT ran on Qwen3.5-4B, which had already been through long-context training. Soft-detached pooling (sd20) beat dense about 4× at small budgets, then lost near 2k PF; the deficit sat at long-range positions and grew with budget. OLMo 3 publishes its stage-2 checkpoint, the point right before its own long-context stage, so long-range learning is what this CPT has to do.

Known limits

  • Dropping tokens breaks OLMo 3's sliding windows unless masked by original position (step 4).
  • Dev CE only so far; generation-graded CTC evals come with the confirm round.
  • 64k FLOP ratios for the drop arms are estimates until their meters report.
  • The forgetting guard comes from OLMo stage-3 training data, so it is a relative check across arms.
