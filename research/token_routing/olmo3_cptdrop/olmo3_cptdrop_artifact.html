Token-dropping continued pretraining on OLMo 3 7B: an empirical screen

Starting from a 7B base that has only ever seen 8k contexts, does dropping tokens during long-context continued pretraining reach dense's dev loss for less compute, at every budget, not only the smallest?

in progress  No results yet. Data build and checkpoint conversion are running; engineering for the drop arms comes next.

Verdict will go here first once round 1 is scored: for each arm, the FLOP multiplier m against the dense curve at 60M, 125M and 250M dense-token budgets.

Why this experiment

Earlier token-drop CPT ran on Qwen3.5-4B, which had already been through long-context training. Soft-detached pooling (sd20) beat dense about 4× at small budgets, then lost near 2k PF; the deficit sat at long-range positions and grew with budget. OLMo 3 publishes its stage-2 checkpoint, the point right before its own long-context stage, so long-range learning is what this CPT has to do.

Selection rule, fixed in advance. m = FLOPs dense needs to reach the arm's dev CE ÷ the arm's FLOPs. An arm wins only with m > 1 at all three budgets, beyond dense seed-to-seed noise.

Known limits

  • Dropping tokens breaks OLMo 3's sliding windows unless masked by original position (step 4).
  • 32k only for now; the long-range regime past 32k is the follow-up.
  • The forgetting guard comes from OLMo stage-3 training data, so it is a relative check across arms.
  • Dev CE only in round 1; generation-graded CTC evals come with the confirm round.
