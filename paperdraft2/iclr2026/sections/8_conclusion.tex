\section{Conclusion / Next Steps}

Our work focuses on clearly defining the challenges around high-\cname settings, identifying the relationship between architecture design and \cname. While helping explain the success of cheaper \cmc{N} methods, our investigation in particular encourages the community to \textit{pay greater attention} to high \cname tasks, which represent an interesting and impactful long-term open problem. Work following up with better CoT strategies, enhancements to mask mixing or applying it to other architectures, creating larger-scale and more realistic benchmarks (see Appendix~\ref{app:realism} for ideas), and systems optimizations to help scale methods are all exciting next steps.
