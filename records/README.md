- [layer-soft-probe.md](layer-soft-probe.md) — **"soft layers": does the pooled-doc soft token have to be applied at EVERY layer?** (eval only, 2026-09-14). Per-layer choice between the full row and the compacted soft row on a fixed dense-trained Qwen3.5-4B. **Answer: no fidelity gain, ~2 points of FLOPs** — against the HEADER-REAL baseline that actually reaches parity, today's every-layer slot already matches full attention (EM 0.865±0.024 vs 0.860±0.025 at 0.738 of FULL's FLOPs) and sparse slot layers only buy marginal compute. **Load-bearing caveat**: the first pass used the header-less slot, which is the known non-parity construction, and produced a spurious "one slot layer dominates" headline plus a spurious mid-stack-cut catastrophe — both retracted in §2/§5. ⚠ probe sizes (eval_size 200 / 64).
- [outlier-slot-probe.md](outlier-slot-probe.md) — **Can a dense model read a POOLED outlier out of its soft-token slot?** (eval only, 2026-09-14). Settles whether the `xhdr*` collapse is an optimisation shortcut or a hard limit, by holding a dense outlier checkpoint fixed and running the training arms' own slot constructions. **Answer: hard limit.** Pooled-gold recall of every header-real construction sits at the `k/n` guess floor, swapping the gold documents' slot vectors with random ones changes nothing (0.094 → 0.099), and the greedy output is a constant `[1], [2], [3]` — while the same rows' REAL-bodied gold is recovered at 0.64–0.69. Keep `kvgb50`; only a richer slot (G > 1, or a trained summarizer) reopens the question. **Qualified 2026-09-15** by [outlier-richer-slot-probe.md](outlier-richer-slot-probe.md): "hard limit" holds for the *plain mean* slot, but a content-only centred mean is readable by an oracle and, once trained, at 2k — outlier is still closed, for the reason in `ds64-handoff.md` §8a. Also documents that **answer-CE parity on outlier does not mean the answer is right**, and a RoPE `position_ids` buffer bug that any generating compacted-path probe will hit. ⚠ eval_size 200 / 64 gen rows; ds64 replication still queued.
- [ds64-handoff.md](ds64-handoff.md) — **READ ME FIRST for the ds64 soft-token data-scaling campaign** (Qwen3.5-4B, short-heavy 2k–64k, dense vs pooled-document soft-KV at matched training FLOPs). Current frontier per task (2026-09-15): oolong `occ00` **0.605 @ 116 PF = dense-16M at 3.60× less compute**, nq `kv33-64M` 0.878 @ 556 PF (2.49×), contradiction `hdr33-32M` 0.810 @ 437 PF (1.74×), outlier **now DOMINATES too, 4.0×** (§8a, 2026-09-15 16:40: `ck64-32M` 0.581 @ 396 PF vs dense-32M 0.564 @ 1582 — the fix was to stop choosing whole documents and keep a uniform real 32–64-token PREFIX of every document beside the slot, §12 `--st-header-extra-tokens`). - [outlier-richer-slot-probe.md](outlier-richer-slot-probe.md) — **Can a cheaper-than-training richer slot make a pooled outlier readable?** (eval only, 2026-09-14/15). 13 slot constructions × 2 checkpoints × 2 corpora. **No construction lifts a frozen dense model off the `k/n` floor** — but an *oracle* cosine readout shows the signal is in the vector all along: `cent_cmean` (content tokens only, minus the centroid) recovers 0.731 at 2k / 0.239 at 8k against floors of 0.225 / 0.053, vs the plain mean's 0.390 / 0.075, at **zero extra tokens and zero extra FLOPs**. So the binding constraint is the READOUT, not the representation — which corrects [outlier-slot-probe.md](outlier-slot-probe.md)'s "hard limit" verdict (true of the *plain* mean only). `G>1` segment means and a block-local encoder slot are both rejected on cost or score. ⚠ eval_size 240/200.
