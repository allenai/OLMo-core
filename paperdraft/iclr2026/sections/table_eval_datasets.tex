\caption{\textbf{Overview of evaluation datasets (22 tasks).} We select datasets that span the full
range of corpus task complexity, from a single pass over the corpus to super-quadratic group search.
Blocks give the \cname{} class; within a block, the category names the capability being tested.
Roughly half the suite are ports of published benchmarks (NaturalQuestions, HotpotQA, BEIR,
MS MARCO, Oolong, OBLIQ-Bench) and half are constructed here, since the high-\cname{} end is
largely unpopulated by existing work. Set-F1, pair-F1 and group-F1 are computed over document IDs,
so every metric but partial credit and Kendall-$\tau$ is scored against an exact ID set; grouping is
additionally scored with ARI. Per-task construction is given in Section~\ref{sec:tasks},
Table~\ref{tab:suite} gives the atomic oracle operation each task decomposes into, and
Appendix~\ref{app:examples} shows a real example of every task.}
