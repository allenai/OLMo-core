## First-attempt failure and correction

Both r1 jobs completed model construction and reached training, then aborted
in the new audit callback after the first step because it required exactly zero
route drops. This was a harness assumption copied from mature small-model
profiling, not an OOM or a failure of the optimized kernels (the optimized arm
had not run). W&B retained finite first/second-step losses around11.788/11.744.
The64GPU first-step maximum block drop fraction was0.0010761693;128GPU was
0.0013224743 (the first offending block in the log was smaller than the maximum).

Historical128GPU medium histories also have nonzero drops, including their
reported final20steps. Mean across23blocks and steps81–100:0.0000095003 for16Mi
and0.0000326115 for8Mi. These are expert-assignment fractions, not whole tokens.
Thus the historical speed numbers are not literally dropless measurements.

The correction changes audit/reporting only, not model, capacity, data, precision,
parallelism or optimization flags. A post-process marker now prevents collectors
from reading a pass before asynchronous metric writes finish. Relaunch into r2
directories; retain failed-run artifacts, and stop obsolete r1 CPU waiters.
